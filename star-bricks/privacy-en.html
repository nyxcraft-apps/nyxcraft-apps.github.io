Starlight Bricks Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 29, 2026 · Announced: November 22, 2026 · Previous policy (effective November 8, 2026)

Policy update notice

We have added Google Analytics for Firebase to receive app usage statistics so we can improve game difficulty and features. The changes are in the Summary, sections 1, 2 and 3, the new section 3-1, and sections 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 and 11. These changes take effect on November 29, 2026. You can turn usage statistics off in the App's settings.

Nyxcraft (닉스크래프트, "we" or "the Company") values the personal information of everyone who uses the Android game app Starlight Bricks (별빛 벽돌깨기, com.nyxcraft.starbricks, "the App") and handles it in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea. This policy explains what information the App stores, where it is stored, and what is sent outside your phone.

This is an English translation of the Korean privacy policy. If the two versions differ, the Korean version prevails.

Summary
  • The App has no account sign-up of its own (you can sign in to Google Play Games only to view the rankings), and we do not operate any servers.
  • Your game records (progress, crystals, settings) are stored on your phone.
  • To improve the game, we receive app usage events such as stage start, clear and fail through Google Analytics for Firebase, and we only view them as statistics that do not identify individuals. You can turn usage statistics off in the App's settings (see section 3-1).
  • If backup is turned on for your phone, Android may back up these records, encrypted, to your own Google Account. We cannot see this backup.
  • To show ads (Google AdMob), Google collects some device information such as your advertising ID (see sections 3 and 4).
  • In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive card numbers or other payment details (see section 5).
  • Rankings are provided by Google Play Games. If you sign in, Google processes your Play Games profile and your score (the total number of stars you have earned). You can play the game without signing in (see section 5).

1. Personal information we collect

The App has no account sign-up feature, and we do not collect information that can identify you, such as your name, email address or phone number. For information collected by the advertising service included in the App (Google AdMob), see sections 3 and 4. For app usage statistics (Google Analytics), see section 3-1. For payments and rankings (Google Play Games), see section 5.

2. Information stored on your phone

The following information is stored in the App's private storage on your phone so you can continue playing. This stored data itself is not sent to us. Information sent as usage statistics is described separately in section 3-1.

  • Game progress: unlocked stages, stars and best score for each stage, and Daily Stage records
  • Number of crystals, ball skins you have bought and the skin you are using, and whether you have bought ad removal
  • Purchase delivery IDs: the IDs of purchases already delivered, so the same purchase is never delivered twice
  • Settings: music, sound effects and vibration on/off, usage statistics on/off, and language (Auto, Korean or English)
  • Whether you have seen the guides for new items and bricks, and the stages where you have received treasure chest crystals

Google Account backup: If backup is turned on for your phone, Android may back up the records above, encrypted, to your own Google Account. This backup is used to restore your records when you reinstall the App or move to a new phone, and the records may also be transferred when you move data directly between phones (by cable or wirelessly). We cannot see this data. You can turn off backup in your phone's settings (for example, Settings › Google › Backup).

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App contains Google AdMob banner ads, full-screen (interstitial) ads, rewarded ads (ads you choose to watch) and a native ad (shown inside the quit confirmation dialog that appears when you press Back to leave the game). To provide ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifiers (advertising ID, app set ID), approximate location (based on IP address), app usage information (app launches, taps, ad views) and diagnostic information (performance information such as app launch time), and use them to show personalized ads, measure ad performance and prevent fraud. We do not pass the game records stored on your phone to advertisers. However, the usage statistics in section 3-1 are linked to our Google AdMob and Google Ads accounts to measure ad revenue and ad performance.

You can find out how Google processes information in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. In some regions (such as Europe), an ad consent screen appears when you first open the App, and you can make your own choice.

3-1. App usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to learn at which stages players get stuck or stop playing, so we can improve difficulty and features, and to measure ad revenue and ad performance. The following information is sent to Google's Firebase servers, and we view it as aggregated statistics (for example, the clear rate for each stage) in Firebase and Google Analytics.

  • Gameplay events: stage number; stage start, clear and fail; turns used; stars earned; whether it was the Daily Stage; use of ad continues and free-ball help; where and how many crystals were spent; crystals received by watching ads; whether new-item guides were seen
  • App usage information (collected automatically): app launches, screen use, usage time, in-app purchases (product, price)
  • Device or other IDs: Firebase app instance ID (a number newly created each time the App is installed), advertising ID
  • Device information: device model, OS version, language, app version
  • Approximate location: country/region level based on IP address (the IP address is partially masked before use)

We do not send your name, email address, phone number or precise location, and we do not attach a user ID of our own. We have not turned on the setting that links activity across devices using your Google Account information (Google signals). Statistics are kept for 14 months and then deleted automatically.

Firebase Analytics is linked to our Google AdMob (ads in the App) and Google Ads (ads promoting the App) accounts. The usage events and advertising ID above are therefore used to measure ad performance: which ad led you to install the App and whether you actually played after installing. The results are used to adjust the ads promoting the App so that they are shown to people likely to enjoy the game.

How to turn it off: Turn off "Send usage statistics" in the App's settings and the App stops sending usage statistics from then on. You can still play the game. If you delete your advertising ID in the ads menu of your phone's settings, your advertising ID is not sent, and clearing the App's data or reinstalling the App creates a new app instance ID. To ask us to delete statistics already sent, please contact us using the details in section 13.

You can find out how Google processes this information in Privacy and Security in Firebase and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Automatic collection tools (advertising ID) and how to refuse

WhatThe Google advertising SDK included in the App (Google AdMob) automatically reads your device's advertising ID and app set ID. Google Analytics for Firebase (section 3-1) also reads your advertising ID and Firebase app instance ID. The App does not use cookies.
WhyPersonalized ads, ad performance measurement and fraud prevention (section 3), app usage statistics (section 3-1)
How to refuseYou can reset or delete your advertising ID in the ads menu of your phone's settings (for example, Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Security and privacy › Privacy › Ads). Menu names may vary slightly by device. You can turn usage statistics off with "Send usage statistics" in the App's settings.
If you refuseYou can still play the game. You may see general ads instead of personalized ads.

5. Google Play (in-app purchases, Play Games rankings)

The App sells crystals and ad removal as in-app products. Payments are processed by the Google Play billing system, and your payment methods and payment history are managed by Google Play. We do not receive card numbers or bank account information. After a purchase is completed, the App receives only purchase confirmation information (product type, purchase ID) from Google Play to deliver the product, and records it on your phone as described in section 2. Payment information is governed by the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Rankings (Google Play Games Services) The App provides rankings based on the total number of stars you have earned through Google Play Games Services. When you open the App, it only checks whether you have signed in before; the sign-in screen appears only when you tap the rankings button. You can keep playing even if you do not sign in or decline to sign in.

  • Information processed: your Play Games profile (gamer ID, nickname, avatar) and the score submitted to the rankings (total stars earned). The Google Play Games SDK may collect analytics and diagnostic information to keep the service stable.
  • Processor: Google. We do not separately receive or keep this information.
  • You decide who can see your profile (everyone, friends, or only you) in your Play Games profile settings.
  • Deletion: you can delete your profile and game data from your Play Games profile or your Google Account.

Play Games information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Permissions

  • Internet, network state: to receive ads and send usage statistics (section 3-1)
  • Vibration: for short vibrations when bricks break or a stage ends (can be turned off in Settings)
  • Advertising ID, ad-related permissions (advertising ID, ad performance measurement, topics), keep awake (WAKE_LOCK), foreground service: used by Google libraries included with the advertising SDK (Google AdMob) and Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): to buy in-app products (crystals, ad removal) through Google Play and to restore products you have bought

The App does not request location, contacts, photos and files, camera or microphone permissions.

8. Sharing with third parties

We do not provide your personal information to third parties. However, information that Google collects and processes directly for advertising (section 3), app usage statistics (section 3-1), and payments and rankings (section 5) is governed by Google's policies.

9. Retention and deletion

  • We do not operate our own servers and do not keep user information on any server.
  • Usage statistics in section 3-1 are kept in Firebase for 14 months and then deleted automatically. If you turn off usage statistics in the App's settings, no more are sent, and you can ask us to delete statistics already sent using the details in section 13.
  • Game records stored on your phone are erased when you uninstall the App or delete its data in your phone's Settings › Apps › Starlight Bricks › Storage.
  • If Google Account backup was turned on, your records may be restored from the backup when you reinstall the App. You can turn off or delete backups in your phone's settings or in Google Drive's backup management.
  • Your ad removal purchase stays on record with Google Play and is restored automatically when you reinstall the App.
  • You can manage information Google has collected for advertising as described in section 4.
  • Ranking scores and your Play Games profile remain in Google Play Games and can be deleted from your Play Games profile or Google Account as described in section 5.

10. Security measures

  • No servers: We do not operate any servers and do not gather the game records stored on your phone on our side. We view the usage statistics in section 3-1 only as statistics that do not identify individuals in Google Analytics.
  • Encrypted transmission: Information exchanged by the Google advertising SDK and Google Analytics for Firebase is encrypted in transit using TLS.
  • App-private storage: Game records are stored in the App's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read.

11. Your rights and how to exercise them

  • You may request access to, correction, deletion or suspension of processing of your personal information at any time. Please contact us using the details in section 13. Because we do not keep user information on a server, you can do most of this yourself as follows.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: section 4
  • Turn off usage statistics: "Send usage statistics" in the App's settings (section 3-1)
  • Delete game records: section 9 (uninstall the App or delete its data in your phone's settings)
  • Ranking scores and Play Games profile: section 5 (Play Games profile or Google Account)
  • For children under 14, a legal guardian may exercise these rights.

12. Children's personal information

The App is not directed at children under 14, and we do not collect personal information from children under 14.
