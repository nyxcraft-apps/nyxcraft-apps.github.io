Starlight Bricks Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

Nyxcraft (닉스크래프트, "we" or "the Company") values the personal information of everyone who uses the Android game app Starlight Bricks (별빛 벽돌깨기, com.nyxcraft.starbricks, "the App") and handles it in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea. This policy explains what information the App stores, where it is stored, and what is sent outside your phone.

This is an English translation of the Korean privacy policy. If the two versions differ, the Korean version prevails.

Summary
  • The App has no account sign-up of its own (you can sign in to Google Play Games only to view the rankings), and we do not operate any servers.
  • Your game records (progress, crystals, settings) are stored on your phone and are not sent to us.
  • If backup is turned on for your phone, Android may back up these records, encrypted, to your own Google Account. We cannot see this backup.
  • To show ads (Google AdMob), Google collects some device information such as your advertising ID (see sections 3 and 4).
  • In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive card numbers or other payment details (see section 5).
  • Rankings are provided by Google Play Games. If you sign in, Google processes your Play Games profile and your score (the total number of stars you have earned). You can play the game without signing in (see section 5).

1. Personal information we collect

The App has no account sign-up feature, and we do not collect information that can identify you, such as your name, email address or phone number. For information collected by the advertising service included in the App (Google AdMob), see sections 3 and 4. For payments and rankings (Google Play Games), see section 5.

2. Information stored on your phone

The following information is stored in the App's private storage on your phone so you can continue playing. It is not sent to us.

  • Game progress: unlocked stages, stars and best score for each stage, and Daily Stage records
  • Number of crystals, ball skins you have bought and the skin you are using, and whether you have bought ad removal
  • Purchase delivery IDs: the IDs of purchases already delivered, so the same purchase is never delivered twice
  • Settings: music, sound effects and vibration on/off, and language (Auto, Korean or English)

Google Account backup: If backup is turned on for your phone, Android may back up the records above, encrypted, to your own Google Account. This backup is used to restore your records when you reinstall the App or move to a new phone, and the records may also be transferred when you move data directly between phones (by cable or wirelessly). We cannot see this data. You can turn off backup in your phone's settings (for example, Settings › Google › Backup).

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App contains Google AdMob banner ads, full-screen (interstitial) ads, rewarded ads (ads you choose to watch) and a native ad (shown inside the quit confirmation dialog that appears when you press Back to leave the game). To provide ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifiers (advertising ID, app set ID), approximate location (based on IP address), app usage information (app launches, taps, ad views) and diagnostic information (performance information such as app launch time), and use them to show personalized ads, measure ad performance and prevent fraud. We do not pass your game records to advertisers.

You can find out how Google processes information in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. In some regions (such as Europe), an ad consent screen appears when you first open the App, and you can make your own choice.

4. Automatic collection tools (advertising ID) and how to refuse

WhatThe Google advertising SDK included in the App (Google AdMob) automatically reads your device's advertising ID and app set ID. The App does not use cookies.
WhyPersonalized ads, ad performance measurement and fraud prevention (section 3)
How to refuseYou can reset or delete your advertising ID in the ads menu of your phone's settings (for example, Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Security and privacy › Privacy › Ads). Menu names may vary slightly by device.
If you refuseYou can still play the game. You may see general ads instead of personalized ads.

5. Google Play (in-app purchases, Play Games rankings)

The App sells crystals and ad removal as in-app products. Payments are processed by the Google Play billing system, and your payment methods and payment history are managed by Google Play. We do not receive card numbers or bank account information. After a purchase is completed, the App receives only purchase confirmation information (product type, purchase ID) from Google Play to deliver the product, and records it on your phone as described in section 2. Payment information is governed by the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Rankings (Google Play Games Services) The App provides rankings based on the total number of stars you have earned through Google Play Games Services. When you open the App, it only checks whether you have signed in before; the sign-in screen appears only when you tap the rankings button. You can keep playing even if you do not sign in or decline to sign in.

  • Information processed: your Play Games profile (gamer ID, nickname, avatar) and the score submitted to the rankings (total stars earned). The Google Play Games SDK may collect analytics and diagnostic information to keep the service stable.
  • Processor: Google. We do not separately receive or keep this information.
  • You decide who can see your profile (everyone, friends, or only you) in your Play Games profile settings.
  • Deletion: you can delete your profile and game data from your Play Games profile or your Google Account.

Play Games information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Permissions

  • Internet, network state: to receive ads
  • Vibration: for short vibrations when bricks break or a stage ends (can be turned off in Settings)
  • Advertising ID, ad-related permissions (advertising ID, ad performance measurement, topics), keep awake (WAKE_LOCK), foreground service: used by Google libraries included with the advertising SDK (Google AdMob)
  • Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): to buy in-app products (crystals, ad removal) through Google Play and to restore products you have bought

The App does not request location, contacts, photos and files, camera or microphone permissions.

7. Transfer of personal information outside Korea

Information collected by Google for advertising (section 3) and rankings (Google Play Games, section 5) is transferred outside Korea as follows.

RecipientGoogle LLC (privacy inquiries)
CountriesThe United States and other countries where Google operates servers
Items transferredAdvertising ID, app set ID, IP address, app usage information, diagnostic information. If you sign in to the rankings, your Play Games profile (gamer ID, nickname, avatar) and score
When and howSent over an encrypted network connection (TLS) when the App loads ads, and when you submit a score to or view the rankings
PurposeProviding ads, measuring ad performance, preventing fraud, providing rankings
Retention periodAs set out in Google's privacy policy
How to refuseYou can reset or delete your advertising ID as described in section 4. You can still play the game after deleting your advertising ID, and you may see general ads instead of personalized ads. Ranking information is not transferred unless you sign in to Play Games.

8. Sharing with third parties

We do not provide your personal information to third parties. However, information that Google collects and processes directly for advertising (section 3) and for payments and rankings (section 5) is governed by Google's policies.

9. Retention and deletion

  • We do not keep user information on any server, so there is no information for you to ask us to delete.
  • Game records stored on your phone are erased when you uninstall the App or delete its data in your phone's Settings › Apps › Starlight Bricks › Storage.
  • If Google Account backup was turned on, your records may be restored from the backup when you reinstall the App. You can turn off or delete backups in your phone's settings or in Google Drive's backup management.
  • Your ad removal purchase stays on record with Google Play and is restored automatically when you reinstall the App.
  • You can manage information Google has collected for advertising as described in section 4.
  • Ranking scores and your Play Games profile remain in Google Play Games and can be deleted from your Play Games profile or Google Account as described in section 5.

10. Security measures

  • No servers: We do not operate any servers and do not gather users' game records on our side.
  • Encrypted transmission: Information exchanged by the Google advertising SDK when loading ads is encrypted in transit using TLS.
  • App-private storage: Game records are stored in the App's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read.

11. Your rights and how to exercise them

  • You may request access to, correction, deletion or suspension of processing of your personal information at any time. Please contact us using the details in section 13. Because we do not keep user information on a server, you can do most of this yourself as follows.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: section 4
  • Delete game records: section 9 (uninstall the App or delete its data in your phone's settings)
  • Ranking scores and Play Games profile: section 5 (Play Games profile or Google Account)
  • For children under 14, a legal guardian may exercise these rights.

12. Children's personal information

The App is not directed at children under 14, and we do not collect personal information from children under 14.
